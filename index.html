Smart Food & Drink ERP

Integrated Manufacturing & Field Sales Management Platform

A comprehensive enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform engineered for food and beverage manufacturers to digitize, streamline, and automate end-to-end factory production, warehousing, and field sales distribution. Designed around real-world manufacturing and FMCG supply chain workflows, the system unifies recipe-based production, batch lot traceability, multi-warehouse inventory, and mobile van sales into one centralized ecosystem—replacing manual spreadsheets and paper invoices with real-time operational intelligence.

01
Production & Lot Batch Tracking

Bill of Materials (BOM) recipe management, production batch scheduling, automated ingredient allocation, and strict batch/lot expiry tracking.

02
Mobile Van Sales

Dedicated mobile application for field sales representatives featuring real-time van stock reconciliation, GPS check-ins, customer route planning, and instant on-the-spot mobile invoicing.

03
Multi-Warehouse Inventory

Live stock movements across central factories and mobile van warehouses, automated Accounts Receivable (AR), payment collection records, and executive revenue dashboards.
